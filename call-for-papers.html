NCAIEE'2024 is a National Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Electrical Engineering, focusing on how AI is used in this field. Its objective is to provide high-quality research and professional interactions for the advancement of science and technology.

National researchers and PhD students were cordially invited to take part in the event.
