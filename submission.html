Information for authors

Authors are encouraged to submit a full paper in English, typically 4 to 6 pages long, containing a comprehensive description of their proposed technical content, following the conference template.

Each paper should address its suitability for the conference topics, demonstrate originality and quality, and exhibit well-organized writing. Final versions of papers are accepted only if the changes requested by the reviewers meet their satisfaction.
