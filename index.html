About the conference

The National Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Electrical Engineering was held on 13 November 2024 at Ouzera Campus, Medea.

Its aim is to facilitate the exchange of ideas and to explore AI applications in Electrical Engineering, bringing together PhD students and researchers from across the country.
